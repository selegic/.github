## Products

### Revenue and quoting

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **[Selegic CPQ](https://github.com/selegic/selegic-cpq-force)** | Configure, price and quote, native on Salesforce: bundles, pricing rules, approvals, quotes, orders, contracts, amendments and renewals. AI agents and MCP tools let Claude and ChatGPT work with quotes. Open source, Apache-2.0. |
| **Selegic Catalog for Salesforce CPQ** | A shopping-style product catalog inside Salesforce CPQ quotes: categories, filters, favorites folders, a mini cart, and frequently added products, then one click adds the picks to the quote. |
| **Selegic Catalog for Revenue Cloud** | The same catalog experience for Salesforce Revenue Cloud (RLM): browse, configure and add products to quotes, with catalog record sync between orgs. |

### Data and documents

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Migrata** | Moves CPQ and Revenue Cloud master data between Salesforce orgs in ordered, schedulable batches, re-linking the references between records on the way. |
| **Doc Gen** | Turns templates into DOCX and PDF documents, from a web app, an API, or inside Salesforce. |

### Business apps

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Selegic CRM** | A configurable CRM: model your own objects, fields and layouts, report and chart, automate with flows, quote with built-in CPQ, and extend it with workspace extensions. |
| **Encye** | A knowledge and learning hub for organizations: articles with review and versioning, trainings and quizzes, communities and short videos, search and recommendations, AI summaries, and an MCP server for AI assistants. |

### Tools

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **[Switch to Classic](https://github.com/selegic/switch-to-classic)** | A Chrome extension that opens the current Lightning page in Salesforce Classic, on the same record, in one click. MIT. [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/selegic-switch-to-classic/ellmmiehdcccalmmdfpoeeablbpnihac) |

## Open source

Linked products have public repositories. **Selegic CPQ** is fully open source under the Apache
License 2.0, with no paid edition: you run it in your own Salesforce org and host its optional
backend and AI agent services yourself. To use any other product, contact us through
[selegic.com](https://selegic.com).

## Get involved

- **Report a bug or ask for a feature:** open an issue in the product's repository.
- **Contribute:** read the repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md` first. AI-assisted contributions are
  welcome when you disclose them and stand behind the result.
- **Report a security problem privately:** follow the repository's `SECURITY.md`. Do not open a
  public issue.
